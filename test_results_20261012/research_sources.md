## 1. Google NotebookLM

- **Developer:** Google Labs (Alphabet Inc.)
- **Official URL:** [https://notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com)
- **Core Technology:** Grounded Gemini 1.5 Pro architecture with user-uploaded document context window.
- **Key Capabilities Verified:**
  - Multi-source document grounding (PDFs, Google Docs, Slides, URLs, audio files).
  - Inline source citations: Every factual response cites specific page and paragraph numbers from uploaded documents.
  - Conversational Audio Overviews: Generates two-host deep-dive audio discussions based strictly on source material.
- **Small Business Application:**
  - Ingestion of vendor contracts, customer service standard operating procedures (SOPs), employee handbooks, and compliance policies.
  - Zero-hallucination internal Q&A for new staff and solo owners.
- **Current Pricing (2026):** Free in Google Labs with personal Google account or Google Workspace.
- **Source Verification:**
  - Google Labs Documentation: *Grounded NotebookLM Research Architecture (2025/2026)*.

## 3. Descript

- **Developer:** Descript, Inc.
- **Official URL:** [https://www.descript.com](https://www.descript.com)
- **Core Technology:** Text-driven multi-track audio and video editing with automatic speech-to-text alignment and neural audio isolation.
- **Key Capabilities Verified:**
  - Text-based video editing: Cutting text in the transcript automatically deletes corresponding video frames.
  - Filler word removal: Detects and removes "ums", "ahs", and repeated filler words with one click.
  - Studio Sound: Deep learning neural network filters out background room reverb, fan noise, and traffic hum.
- **Small Business Application:**
  - Creating video marketing demos, social reels, YouTube content, and client walkthroughs in minutes rather than spending hours in complex timeline editors.
- **Current Pricing (2026):**
  - Free Tier: Basic editing and transcription allowance (1 hour/month).
  - Creator Plan: ~\$12–\$15/month billed annually.
  - Pro Plan: ~\$24/month billed annually.
- **Source Verification:**
  - Descript Official Features & Studio Sound Documentation.
  - Descript Creator & Pro Pricing Tier Matrix (2025/2026).
